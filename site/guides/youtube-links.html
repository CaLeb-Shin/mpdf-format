When a YouTube link doesn't play

YouTube tracks play through YouTube's own embedded player, so when one stays silent the cause is almost always one of the items below. Work down the list.

1. The link was a playlist or a channel

The maker accepts video links only: youtube.com/watch?v=…, youtu.be/… and Shorts. Open the video itself and copy that address. A playlist link is refused with “That's not a YouTube link”.

2. “Video unavailable” inside the player

The uploader has turned off embedding, or the video is private, deleted, or blocked in your country. Nothing on our side can override that. Search for another upload of the same piece; official channels usually allow embedding.

3. Nothing happened when the page opened

That is by design. Browsers do not allow sound before you tap something, so the first page's track is only cued. Tap the pill (or the play button) once; after that, page turns switch tracks on their own.

4. The player is visible and you'd like it hidden

YouTube's terms require the player to stay on screen while it plays, so MPDF viewers keep it inside the popup rather than hiding it. Collapse the popup to a pill and the video shrinks with it; the audio keeps going.

5. Age-restricted videos

These require a signed-in YouTube session and often refuse to play embedded. Choose a different upload.

6. The file opens as a plain PDF with no player

The playlist rides inside the PDF as an attachment. If the file was passed through “Print to PDF”, a screenshot, or some compressor or markup tools, the attachment was dropped and only the pages survived. Ask the sender for the original file, or for a link.

7. It plays in the web viewer but not in another app

That app doesn't support MPDF yet: it opens the file as a normal PDF, which is exactly what the format promises. Open the file in the web viewer, or point the app's developer to the spec; adding support is a small job.

8. Sound on a phone

Check the volume, and on iPhone try the ring/silent switch. Bluetooth speakers connected to another device are a classic.
